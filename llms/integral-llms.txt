# Integral

> Integral (Integral Development Corp.) is a technology provider for institutional currency and digital-asset trading. Its cloud-native, API-first platform delivers liquidity aggregation, execution management, pricing engines, distribution, risk management, settlement, and market data across FX, CFDs, precious metals, and crypto. It is SOC 2 Type II certified and connects via FIX, ITCH, and proprietary REST and WebSocket APIs.

## Products

- [Integral FX](https://www.integral.com/products/fx/): Trading automation for institutional foreign exchange, CFDs, and precious metals.
- [Integral Digital](https://www.integral.com/products/digital/): Institutional-grade connectivity and execution for digital/crypto assets.
- [PrimeOne](https://www.primeone.net/): Stablecoin-based prime brokerage platform for cryptocurrency.

## APIs

Integral's trading APIs (FIX, ITCH, proprietary REST, and WebSocket) are provisioned to institutional clients through onboarding; there is no public developer portal, OpenAPI document, or self-serve API key. Request access via the demo/contact flow at https://www.integral.com/demo/.
