Sam Chaney, Commercial Director at M4Markets spoke with Andrew Saks about one of the most important strategic questions in brokerage today: whether a brokerage should build their own trading infrastructure or buy off-the-shelf technology. The answer is rarely simple. Speed to market, cost, scalability, vendor control, and long-term flexibility all play a part.
